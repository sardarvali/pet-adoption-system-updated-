Adoption Process

Your step-by-step guide to bringing home your new family member

  1. Home
  2. Adoption Process

Browse and Select a Pet

The journey begins with finding your perfect match

Browse Online

Explore our extensive database of pets available for adoption. Filter by species, breed, age, size, and location to find pets that match your lifestyle.

Save Favorites

Create an account to save pets you're interested in and receive updates when new pets matching your preferences become available.

Ask Questions

Contact shelter staff with questions about specific pets before applying. They can provide detailed information about temperament, health, and compatibility.

Complete an Application

Tell us about yourself and your home

Application Form

Submit an online application for your chosen pet. The form includes questions about your living situation, experience with pets, and lifestyle to ensure a good match.

Application Review

Our team reviews applications within 24-48 hours. You'll receive updates on your application status through your account dashboard.

Initial Interview

If your application passes the initial review, we'll contact you for a phone interview to discuss the adoption in more detail.

Meet and Greet

Get to know your potential new family member

Schedule a Visit

After your application is approved, schedule a time to meet the pet at their shelter or foster home location.

Bring Family Members

We recommend bringing all household members to ensure everyone connects with the pet. For dog adoptions, bringing resident dogs is often required.

Interaction Time

Spend time interacting with the pet to see if their personality and energy level match your expectations. Staff will be available to answer questions and provide guidance.

Home Check

Ensuring your home is ready for a new pet

Virtual or In-Person Check

For some adoptions, particularly for certain breeds or special needs pets, a home check may be required. This can often be done virtually through a video call.

Safety Assessment

Our staff will check that your home is safe for the pet, with secure fencing for dogs if applicable, and no obvious hazards.

Preparation Guidance

We'll provide guidance on how to prepare your home for your new pet, including recommended supplies and pet-proofing tips.

Finalize the Adoption

Complete paperwork and prepare to welcome your pet

Adoption Agreement

Sign the adoption contract which outlines your responsibilities as a pet owner and our commitment to support you.

Adoption Fee

Pay the adoption fee which helps cover the cost of care, vaccinations, microchipping, and spay/neuter procedures.

Medical Records

Receive all medical records, including vaccination history, microchip information, and any special care instructions.

Bringing Your Pet Home

The beginning of your new life together

Transportation

Bring a secure carrier for small animals and cats. For dogs, bring a proper leash and collar with ID tags.

Settling In

Allow your new pet time to adjust to their new environment. We'll provide guidance on introducing them to other pets and family members.

Ongoing Support

Our team remains available to answer questions and provide support throughout your pet's life. Follow-up check-ins are scheduled at 2 weeks, 1 month, and 3 months post-adoption.

Frequently Asked Questions

Adoption fees vary depending on the type and age of the pet:

  • Dogs: $150-$350
  • Puppies (under 1 year): $250-$450
  • Cats: $75-$175
  • Kittens (under 1 year): $125-$225
  • Small animals: $25-$75
  • Birds: $50-$150

These fees help cover vaccinations, microchipping, spay/neuter procedures, and basic medical care the animal received while in our care.

The adoption process typically takes 3-7 days from application to bringing your pet home. The timeline depends on several factors:

  • Application review: 24-48 hours
  • Scheduling meet and greet: 1-2 days
  • Home check (if required): 1-2 days
  • Finalizing paperwork: Same day as final approval

For some special cases or high-demand pets, the process may take longer. We prioritize finding the right match over speed.

Yes, renters can adopt pets! We'll need to verify that your lease allows pets and may request:

  • A copy of your lease showing pet policies
  • Written permission from your landlord
  • Information about pet deposits or fees you've paid

Many landlords have restrictions on pet type, size, or breed, so please confirm these details before applying for specific pets.

We understand that sometimes, despite best efforts, an adoption may not be the right fit. Our return policy includes:

  • 14-day trial period during which the full adoption fee is refunded
  • 30-day partial refund period (50% of adoption fee)
  • Lifetime return policy - we will always take our animals back rather than have them rehomed elsewhere

We also offer post-adoption support including training resources and behavioral counseling to help work through adjustment issues.

Yes, we offer several programs to make adoption more accessible:

  • Senior-to-Senior program: Reduced fees for seniors (65+) adopting senior pets (7+ years)
  • Veteran adoption program: 25% discount for military veterans
  • Special needs pet support: Additional resources and sometimes reduced fees for pets with medical or behavioral needs
  • Payment plans: Available on a case-by-case basis

Contact our adoption counselors to learn more about these programs and determine eligibility.
